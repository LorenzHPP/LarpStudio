Using Larp Studio

01Connect your iPhoneConnect by USB, unlock your iPhone and trust this PC. Developer Mode must be enabled.

02Choose a placeClick the map, drag the pin or search for a place. For a route, add several points and select “Plan route”.

03Start your journeyDriving uses road-aware speed by default: speed and travel time follow each road section. Walking and running use your chosen speed. Pause holds your position. Stop sends the reset command.

Check the simulation in Apple Maps first. Snapchat may show a different location. If a simulated location remains after stopping, try resetting and restart your iPhone if needed.
Map, internet & data

Map tiles: OpenStreetMap. Search: Photon/komoot. Routes: FOSSGIS. Searches and route points are sent to these services for processing. Saved places stay on your PC. No sign-in required.

Connect over Wi-Fi

01Same networkConnect your PC and iPhone to your home network. Your PC can use Ethernet on the same router. Guest networks may isolate devices.

02Prepare once over USBConnect and unlock your iPhone. Larp Studio prepares the developer connection for this trusted iPhone and enables its Wi-Fi connections.

03Check Wi-Fi, then unplugAfter setup, select “Connect”. Unplug USB and start the simulation only after the Wi-Fi connection check succeeds.

Your PC and Larp Studio must stay running. Leaving the network interrupts the session. This method cannot reliably continue a route without a connection to the PC. Compatibility with your iOS 27.2 still needs testing.
